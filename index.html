Who are you bringing in?

A 60-second prototype of how a founder could invite a co-founder, an employee, an accountant or an AI agent, give each exactly the access they need, and never expose banking details by accident. Pick a person, adjust what they can do, and watch their view change.

What they'll see

Start from the person

Founders think "my accountant", not "export_statements". Roles come first, permissions are the fine print.

Least access by default

Every preset starts narrow, adding access is one switch, and full account numbers stay masked unless someone truly needs them.

Agents get a job, not a login

An agent is scoped to one task, capped on money, set to expire, and every action it takes is logged and reversible.
